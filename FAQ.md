# FAQ

Site: https://yuicoder.github.io/VoxStream/

The old URL `/Voxlive/` is dead.

## I hear nothing

The browser blocks audio until a click. Press **Test voice** once. Unmute the tab.
Firefox on Windows only lists Microsoft voices. Chrome on the same PC lists more.

## Twitch chat is empty

The channel has to be live. Type the name without `#`, then **Connect**. No password.
Or turn on **Rehearsal**.

## Is there an installer?

No exe. It is a website.

## Do I need an account?

No. Free does not need an account.

## How do I log in?

GitHub or Google. No password form.

- https://voxstream-production.up.railway.app/v1/auth/github
- https://voxstream-production.up.railway.app/v1/auth/google

## How do I get Plus or Pro?

Ko-fi shop orders at https://ko-fi.com/bloodybytes/shop grant Plus or Pro.
Use the same email as GitHub/Google. Stream tips, bits, and subs do not grant.

## Is live Stripe on?

No. Plus/Pro come from the Ko-fi shop.

## How do I test the studio?

1. Unmute the tab. Click **Test voice**.
2. Live Twitch channel, **Connect**.
3. **Rehearsal** if you are not live.
4. **Stage** for OBS (`studio.html#stage`).
