FAQ

Where is the site?

https://yuicoder.github.io/VoxStream/

The old URL /Voxlive/ is dead. Use VoxStream.

I hear nothing

Chrome blocks audio until a click. Press Probar voz once. Unmute the Chrome tab.

TikTok chat is empty

The LIVE has to be open. Get a free Euler key at eulerstream.com/register. Paste it, username without @, Connect. No key: use Demo.

Is there an installer?

No exe. It is a website. Open the studio in Chrome.

Do I need an account?

No. Free does not need an account.

How do I log in?

GitHub or Google in the nav, or:

https://voxstream-production.up.railway.app/v1/auth/github

https://voxstream-production.up.railway.app/v1/auth/google

No password form.

How do I get Plus or Pro?

Ko-fi shop orders at https://ko-fi.com/bloodybytes/shop grant Plus or Pro. Use the same email as GitHub/Google. Stream tips, bits, and subs do not grant.

Is live Stripe on?

No. Stripe live is closed. Plus/Pro come from the Ko-fi shop.

How do I test the studio?

  1. Voice: unmute, Probar voz.
  2. Twitch: live channel, Connect.
  3. TikTok Free: your Euler key + LIVE, or Demo.
  4. Stage for OBS.
